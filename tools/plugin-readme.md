Build your Digital Home with a team of AI employees, right inside Buzz. Start with Simon, your Digital Home Manager, then recruit specialists for brand strategy, copywriting, funnels, content and video as your membership allows.

Each employee arrives with BraveBrand training for their role. Recruit them into your current workspace and continue the conversation in Buzz.

## Getting started

1. Connect Goose, Claude or Codex in **Settings → Agent setup** before recruiting. With Goose, hires use the provider and model you set in Buzz.
2. Open **BraveBrand Team** from the sidebar.
3. Paste your **access link** from the Skool classroom.
4. Start with **Simon**: click **Employ Me**, find him in **Agents**, and say hello.

## Your membership

**Standard** includes Simon. **Premium** unlocks all six available employees. The Sales Manager is still in training and is not yet available.

Already Premium? Open a locked employee and choose **Already Premium? Enter your access link**. Paste your Premium link to unlock the team in the same plugin.

## Already have your team?

Keep your existing employees and conversations. This plugin does not replace their training or automatically update them. If an employee you already have is not recognised, ask for help before hiring another copy.
