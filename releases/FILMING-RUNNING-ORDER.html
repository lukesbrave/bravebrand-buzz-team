BRAVEBRAND / RECORDING NOTES

Your first AI employee.

One course update: set up Buzz One Beta, find your way around, add BraveBrand Team and recruit Simon. Finish at his first reply. Build the Home in the next lesson.

Aim: 10–15 minutes. Keep this guide off the recorded screen.

Before you press record

The GitHub import on this fresh Mac user is still worth rehearsing before the take. Earlier native checks used the local-folder route. Don’t delete or rehire existing agents to create a demonstration.

01 · Explain the destination

30–45 sec
  • Buzz is your office. Your agents are your employees. Your Digital Home is the business asset you own.
  • You need Buzz, a connected AI provider and the BraveBrand classroom resources.

“By the end of this lesson, you’ll have Buzz set up and Simon ready to help you build your Digital Home. I’m using Claude for this walkthrough—that’s the setup we’ve tested for recruiting your team.”

02 · Download and set up Buzz

3–4 min
  • Show the classroom’s Buzz One Beta download and installation on your Mac.
  • Complete identity setup and explain saving recovery details privately. Pause or cut the recording while secrets are visible.
  • Connect and authorise Claude. Confirm it is ready in Buzz.
  • Create or select the business workspace/community. Explain “community” means their working space here.
  • Set a name and profile picture if the setup asks for them.

“Keep your recovery details somewhere safe. Don’t share them in the classroom or send them to an agent.”

03 · Give the quick office tour

1–2 min
  • Show where conversations, channels and Agents live.
  • Open Settings and show colours, appearance and light/dark mode briefly.
  • Point out Plugins. This is where they add the BraveBrand recruiting page.
  • Skip advanced automations, hosting and model settings in this lesson.

04 · Add BraveBrand Team from GitHub

1–2 min
  • Open Settings → Plugins → Manage plugins → Add.
  • Paste the repository URL and the branch separately. Do not leave the branch blank.
  • Click Find, select BraveBrand Team in dist, review access, then install/enable it.
  • Open BraveBrand Team in the sidebar. No individual JSON download is needed.

Git or GitHub repository

https://github.com/lukesbrave/bravebrand-buzz-team Branch or tagcodex/hire-plugin-fixes-2026-10-31

These are the prepared branch details. Keep them in editable lesson text. The default branch is older; do not paste a GitHub /tree/ URL.

05 · Activate Standard and hire Simon

1–2 min
  • Copy the Standard access link from the classroom and paste it into the plugin; conceal the link in the recording.
  • Explain that Buzz remembers the link in that profile. A new Mac user or device may need it again.
  • Introduce Simon, the Digital Home Manager, and click Employ Me.
  • Find Simon in Agents and open his conversation.

“Simon is your first hire. He’ll guide you through setting up your Digital Home, step by step.”

06 · Show the Premium path

45–60 sec
  • Open a locked employee to show Want the full team? and Upgrade to Premium in Skool.
  • Show Already Premium? Enter your access link; conceal the actual Premium link while entering it.
  • Explain: same plugin, different access link. No second installation.
  • Standard includes Simon. Premium unlocks all six available employees. The Sales Manager is not available yet.
  • Show the unlocked team without hiring everyone during this lesson.

07 · Start the first conversation and wrap

1 min
  • Message Simon with the new-Home introduction below. Show his first response.
  • For existing members: tell Simon about the Home they already have and ask him to inspect it before making changes.
  • Point to the next lesson: building the Digital Home.

“Hi Simon. I’m new here and want to build my Digital Home from scratch. Please guide me through the first step.”

“That’s your first AI employee hired. In the next lesson, we’ll start building your Digital Home together.”
