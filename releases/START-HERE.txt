BRAVEBRAND TEAM — START HERE

1. Download and unzip this package.
   On Mac, double-click the ZIP. On Windows, choose Extract All.
   Keep the extracted BraveBrand Team folder somewhere you can find it.

2. Open Buzz One Beta and complete its setup.
   Connect Claude in Buzz's agent setup before recruiting your first agent.
   This recruiting version requires the local Claude connection.

3. Open Settings > Plugins > Manage plugins > Add.
   Choose the folder option, then select the extracted "BraveBrand Team"
   folder containing manifest.json and plugin.js. Do not select the ZIP.
   Review the plugin details and access, then install/enable it.
   This package is unsigned; obtain it only from the BraveBrand classroom.

4. Open BraveBrand Team from Buzz's navigation.
   Copy your access link from the Skool classroom and paste it into the plugin.
   Standard and Premium members use the same plugin download.

5. Start with Simon, your Digital Home Manager.
   Click Employ Me, find him in Agents, and say hello.
   Continue with the Digital Home setup lesson.

ALREADY HAVE YOUR AGENTS?
Keep your existing agents and conversations. Do not hire another copy.
This plugin does not update or replace their training.
If an existing agent is not recognised, stop and ask for help before hiring.

UPGRADING TO PREMIUM?
Open a locked agent, choose "Already Premium? Enter your access link",
then paste your Premium link from the classroom.
You do not need another plugin download.
Your link is remembered in that Buzz profile; a new device/profile may need it again.
